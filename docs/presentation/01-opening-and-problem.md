# 01 — Opening and Problem

**Speaker:** Presenter 1  
**Target time:** ~1.5 minutes

## What to say (exact sentences)

"Hello everyone, we are presenting EcoPrompt."  
"EcoPrompt helps people choose AI models more responsibly by showing the resource impact before and after a request."  
"The problem is that users usually see only the AI answer, but not the hidden resource cost of model choice."  
"Because of this, people may pick a large model even when a smaller model is enough for the task."  
"Our goal is not to block users, but to make model choice more visible, explainable, and informed."  
"EcoPrompt classifies prompt complexity, recommends a model size, and shows token and carbon estimates with uncertainty."  
"Next, Presenter 2 will show the live flow in the app."

## Slide/demo cues

- Show title slide: **EcoPrompt: Carbon-Aware AI Prompting**.
- Show one problem slide: hidden resource cost and model over-selection risk.
- Keep this section short and transition directly to the demo.
